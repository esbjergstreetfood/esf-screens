ESF sharpness test

measuring…

1 — Line gratings

Vertical stripes drawn at exact device-pixel widths. Photograph these close up. If the 1 px grating shows as clean alternating lines, the page really is rasterising at device resolution. If it shows as flat grey, moiré, or uneven banding, it is being scaled.

2 — The same text, three ways

Identical string, identical size, three different paths to the panel. Compare them against each other, not against memory.

A · HTML TEXT (browser rasterises the glyphs)
Margherita DKK 89,-
Quattro Formaggi · San Marzano tomato sauce
Mascarpone, portobello mushrooms, mozzarella, artichoke hearts & parmesan
Homemade white sauce, parmesan & slow-cooked homemade beef ragu
B · CANVAS TEXT (drawn at devicePixelRatio)
C · IMAGE (same canvas, exported to PNG, shown as <img>)

3 — Hairlines

One-device-pixel rules at increasing spacing. Any that vanish, double up, or vary in weight mean the surface is being resampled.
